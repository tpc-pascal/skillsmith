# Slop scoring — self-audit before delivery

Distilled from [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) @ `8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9` (MIT, Hardik Pandya). Score a draft before delivering it; the pattern catalogue itself lives in [`ai-writing-tells.md`](ai-writing-tells.md).

## Checklist

1. Remove throat-clearing and hedging openers — start at the point.
2. Avoid templated AI sentence structures — rewrite, do not word-swap.
3. Stop inanimate subjects acting like people ("the report underscores…", "the data reveals…") unless the metaphor earns its place.
4. Drop absolutes ("every", "always", "never") paired with vague scope, and filler quantifiers ("many", "several", "various").
5. Drop the narrator-from-a-distance ("people say…", "it is widely believed…") — cite a source or delete.
6. Break rule-of-three triplets that the meaning does not require.
7. Drop hand-holding asides ("It is worth noting…", "It is important to remember…").
8. No pull-quote or moral-of-the-story endings — finish on information.

## Rubric

| Criterion | What a 10 looks like |
|---|---|
| Directness | No fluff or throat-clearing; point first |
| Rhythm | Varied sentence lengths, no mechanical pattern |
| Trust | Concrete data and named sources, no vague generalities |
| Authenticity | Plain language; zero marketing speak |
| Density | High information-to-word ratio |

Score each criterion 1–10. **Total below 35/50 → revise and re-score before delivering.**
